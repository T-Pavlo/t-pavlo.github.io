Hindsight Corp. — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Hindsight Corp. ("we") runs the YouTube channel @HindsightCorp and a private internal tool ("Hindsight uploader") that uploads our own videos to our own YouTube channel.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos, set thumbnails and manage playlists on the Hindsight Corp. channel. It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to the public.

What data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any users or viewers. The only credential the app holds is the channel owner's own OAuth token, stored securely and used only to access the owner's own channel.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy (https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services: see the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
